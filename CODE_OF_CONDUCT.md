# Community code of conduct

DialyCore welcomes contributors with different professional, technical, cultural, and lived experiences. We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and privacy-conscious community.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, clarity, and professional respect.
- Give actionable feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty, especially across clinical and technical disciplines.
- Distinguish local practice, personal experience, and validated requirements.
- Protect privacy and use independently authored synthetic examples only.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or sexualized conduct
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sharing real or derived patient, staff, institutional, credential, or operational data
- Presenting the project as certified, clinically validated, or universally applicable
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or contribution processes

## Reporting

Report community concerns privately to the repository owner. Report security issues or accidental sensitive-data disclosures through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never through a public issue.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community or the project.

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone is representing DialyCore in public.
